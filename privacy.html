Lucky Sprout privacy policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

Lucky Sprout is a gardening game. This policy explains what the game stores, why, and how to delete it.

What we store

  • An anonymous player account. On first launch the game creates an anonymous account with Google Firebase. It is a random ID, not linked to your name, email or phone number.
  • Your public garden. So other players can visit it and see the rankings, the game publishes your garden: its name (put together from a fixed word list, never typed text), your crops, pets, decorations, level and scores such as coins earned. Other players can see this.
  • Likes and waterings. When you like or water a garden, the game records your anonymous ID and garden name with it, so each player can only like a garden once and water it once a day, and the owner can see who watered.
  • Market sales and shared strains. How many of each crop players sell each hour is counted together, without saying who sold them, so prices can follow supply. Hybrid strains you choose to share in the Seed Bank are published with your anonymous ID, their parent crops and star ratings, and how many packs have sold.
  • A cloud backup of your game. Your game progress is backed up to Firebase under a restore code shown in Settings, so you can bring it back on a new phone. Anyone who knows that code can restore the garden, so keep it private.
  • Game Center. If you use Game Center, Apple shares your scores with its leaderboards under Apple's own privacy policy.
  • Purchases. Coin packs are bought through Apple. We never see your payment details.

We do not collect your name, email address, location, contacts or advertising identifiers. There are no ads and no tracking across other apps or websites.

How it's used

Only to run the game: showing gardens to visit, the rankings, likes, and restoring your progress. We don't sell or share your data with anyone else.

Where it's kept

In Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore and Firebase Authentication), which stores data on Google's servers.

Deleting your data

In the game, open Settings → Delete my account. This deletes your published garden, your shared strains, your cloud backup, your anonymous account and the game on your phone. It can't be undone.

Children

Lucky Sprout doesn't ask for personal information, and players can't send each other messages or free text.

Contact

Questions about this policy: contact us, or see our support page.
