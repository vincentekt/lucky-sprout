You'll need a free GitHub account to post. Please don't include your restore code: it lets anyone take over your garden.

Moving to a new phone

Open Settings (the gear) on your old phone and note your restore code. On the new phone, open Settings, enter the code under Restore a garden, and tap Restore.

A purchase didn't arrive

Coin packs are added as soon as Apple confirms them. Close and reopen the game while online; if they still haven't arrived, contact us with the date of purchase.

Deleting your data

Open Settings → Delete my account. Your garden, cloud backup and account are deleted for good.
